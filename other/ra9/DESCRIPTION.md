# Turf Wars: the store description

Roblox shows only the first line or two in search and on the game tile, so the hook goes first. Every word is
plain enough for a third grader.

## Paste this

```
Every hit steals their floor. Don't let them take yours! 🏹

🔴 RED vs BLUE 🔵
Your side of the floor is your turf. Every time you knock someone out, the line moves and you take more of theirs.
Push it all the way to their base to win!

🧱 BUILD, THEN FIGHT
You get 20 seconds to build walls, towers and ramps. Then the fight starts!

🏹 PICK YOUR WEAPON
• Bow: hold to pull back, let go. One hit, one knockout!
• TNT: hold to wind up, then throw it. BOOM! Walls fly apart!
• The CHARGE: in the last 20 seconds of every fight the line opens. Run across, blade out, and swat arrows right back!

🏃 MOVE LIKE A PRO
Run, slide, crouch, and flip off your fourth jump.

🛡️ SAFE SPAWNS
Nobody can hurt you in your base until you shoot. Get ready, then charge!

🤖 NO ONE ELSE ON? A bot fights you until a friend joins.

🎁 Win to unlock new bows, trails and effects. Get the Turf Pass for even more!

Controls: Click to shoot · 1 2 3 or scroll to switch · Shift to run · C to crouch · B for the shop
```

## Other hooks to try (swap the first line)

* `Your side is getting smaller. Can you push it back? 🏹`
* `They're taking your floor. Take it back! 🏹`
* `Every hit moves the line. Which side will win? 🏹`

The first line is the one to test: change it every week or two and keep the one that brings more visits.

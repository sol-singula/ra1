# Turf Wars: the icon

![Main icon and two alternates](_all.jpg)

**512 × 512 PNG.** `icon_main.png` is the one to use: the arena from above, split into red and blue turf, an archer
of each team on the line, tracers crossing. It says the game at a glance, even small. `icon_archer_alt.png` (a red
archer drawing straight at you over the red and blue line) and `icon_arrows_alt.png` (a red and a blue arrow
colliding) are alternates for an icon test. Made like the thumbnails (Higgsfield, GPT Image 2.5, from the game's
renders), no text.

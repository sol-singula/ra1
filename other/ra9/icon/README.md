# Turf Wars: the icon

![Main icon and five alternates](_all.jpg)

**512 × 512 PNG, no text.** One bold subject that still reads at tiny size, and a "wait, what?" that gets the click.

| File | Picture | Why it gets the click |
|---|---|---|
| `icon_main.png` (use this) | One player split down the middle, half red and half blue, a bow in one hand and a blade in the other | Strange at first look: who is this? It shows red vs blue, bows and blades |
| `icon_blade_clash.png` | A red and a blue player clash blades over the line, sparks flying | Pure action: who wins? |
| `icon_last_tile.png` | From above: one blue player on the last blue tile, red archers closing in | Can he get out? |
| `icon_behind_the_wall.png` | A blue archer peeks round a wall; a red one waits on the other side with a blade | He doesn't know! |
| `icon_tower.png` | Looking up a huge white tower, a tiny blue player planting a flag on top | Wonder and scale |
| `icon_aimed_at_you.png` | A red archer behind a wall, the arrow pointing out of the icon | Danger, close up |

Run the alternates in Roblox's icon test against the main one and keep the winner. The split player isn't a real
character in the game (everyone is one colour); it stands for the two sides. Made with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5,
high, 2k) using the game's art and renders as references, cut with `title_art.py --icon`.

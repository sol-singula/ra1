# HOLD THE CASTLE: Dwarves vs Monsters — The Game Plan

**Build a giant castle with your friends. Then hold it when the monsters come.**

This is our plan for the whole game. It uses simple words so the whole team can read it fast.

## 1. The Big Idea

- Everyone starts as a dwarf.
- Dwarves build a castle around a magic shrine.
- Then the plague comes. Some dwarves turn into monsters.
- Monsters attack the castle. Dwarves fight back.
- When a dwarf dies, they turn into a monster too.
- Monsters try to take the shrine. Dwarves run back to the next shrine.
- The last shrine is the last stand.
- Monsters almost always win in the end. Dwarves win by holding on as long as they can.
- The game is bright and full of color.
- It runs great on phones.
- It plays like the old Minecraft game Dwarves vs. Zombies. But it looks brand new.

## 2. The Name

Top pick: **HOLD THE CASTLE: Dwarves vs Monsters**

Why this name:

- It tells you what to do in three words. Hold the castle.
- It names both teams. Dwarves. Monsters.
- A kid can read it.
- It sounds like a dare. Can YOU hold it?
- It works on a small phone screen.
- It leaves room for new maps and new monsters later.

Other names we like:

- Build & Defend: Dwarf Castle — Shows the two things you do. Build. Defend.
- Dwarf Siege — Short and cool. But "siege" is a hard word for little kids.
- Last Dwarf Standing — Shows the last stand. But not the building part.
- Castle Siege: Build & Survive — Clear, but a bit long.
- Fortress Fall — Our old pick. Sounds great. But it does not say what you do.

The short line under the name:

- **Build it. Hold it. Never give up.**

To do before we lock it in:

- Check if the name is free on Roblox.
- Check if another game already uses it.
- Check if someone owns the name.

## 3. Who Is This Game For?

- Kids and teens who play Roblox.
- Phone players first. PC and tablet too.
- Friends who want to play together.
- Players who like to build.
- Players who like to fight.
- Players who like to sneak and trick people.
- Fans of the old Dwarves vs. Zombies game.
- Fans of castle games and tower games.

## 4. One Full Match, Step by Step

### Step 1: The Shrine Shows Up

- The match starts.
- Everyone is a dwarf.
- You see one glowing shrine in a field.
- You see rocks, trees, and mines close by.
- You see the paths the monsters will use.
- A big sign says: HELP BUILD THE CASTLE.

### Step 2: Build Time (about 8 to 10 minutes)

- Dwarves pick a job.
- Dwarves dig for gold, stone, and wood.
- Dwarves put up walls, gates, and towers.
- Dwarves make armor, weapons, food, and potions.
- Dwarves set traps.
- The castle gets bigger and bigger.
- A clock counts down. Everyone can see it.

### Step 3: The Plague

- The sky turns dark.
- A horn blows.
- Some dwarves get sick and turn into monsters.
- Those players are now on the monster team.
- They know where the castle is weak. That makes them scary.

### Step 4: The Attack

- Monsters come out of their spawn.
- Monsters walk the paths to the castle.
- Monsters smash walls and gates.
- Dwarves shoot, swing, and fix things.
- Monsters get stronger as the fight goes on.
- Dead dwarves turn into monsters.
- The monster team grows. The dwarf team shrinks.

### Step 5: Taking a Shrine

- Monsters stand near a shrine.
- If there are more monsters than dwarves near it, the shrine drains.
- A big bar shows how close the shrine is to falling.
- Dwarves can rush in to stop it.
- When the shrine falls, monsters spawn there now.
- Dwarves run back to the next shrine.

### Step 6: The Last Stand

- The last shrine is the last hope.
- A few dwarves are left. Lots of monsters are coming.
- Walls are broken. Food is low.
- This is the part players tell their friends about.
- When the last shrine falls, the match ends.

### Step 7: Rewards

- Everyone sees how long the dwarves held on.
- Everyone sees how many monsters they beat.
- Everyone gets coins and XP.
- Then a new match starts fast.

## 5. Match Numbers (our first guess, we will test and change)

- Players per match: 16 to 24.
- Build time: 8 to 10 minutes.
- Fight time: 10 to 15 minutes.
- Whole match: about 20 to 25 minutes.
- Shrines: 3.
- Dwarf jobs at launch: 5.
- Monster jobs at launch: 5.
- The plague turns about 1 in 4 dwarves at first.

Rules for these numbers:

- These are guesses. Not facts.
- We test with real players.
- We change the numbers until both teams have fun.
- We write down every change and why.

## 6. The Map

### The 5 parts every map needs

- **The shrines.** Three of them in a row. Monsters want them. Dwarves guard them.
- **The build zone.** Big open space around each shrine. Dwarves build here.
- **Monster spawn.** Where monsters start. Far from the first shrine.
- **Monster paths.** Trails, bridges, tunnels, and fields that lead to the castle.
- **Mines and woods.** Where dwarves get gold, stone, and wood.

### Map rules

- The shrine must be easy to see. Never hide it.
- There must be more than one path to the castle.
- Dwarves can block paths. But they can never block them all forever.
- A thin bridge is easy to guard but easy to break.
- An open field is hard to guard but has room for towers.
- Mines are outside the walls. Going out is a risk. That is the point.
- Each shrine has a safe zone. Monsters cannot skip ahead to the next shrine early.
- The map gets smaller as shrines fall. The fight moves forward.

### Our first map

- One map only at launch.
- A green valley with a stone hill in the middle.
- Shrine 1 on the open hill.
- Shrine 2 inside a rocky canyon.
- Shrine 3 deep inside a mountain cave.
- Monster spawn in a dark forest at the edge.
- Mines in the hills on the sides.

### Later maps

- Snowy mountain pass.
- Desert ruins.
- Swamp with wooden bridges.
- Sky islands with rope bridges.
- Crystal cave under the ground.

## 7. Building the Castle

### How building works

- Players collect stone, wood, and gold.
- Players carry it to a build spot.
- A ghost shape shows where the wall will go.
- Tap to place it. It snaps into place on a grid.
- Walls rise up with dust, hammer sounds, and sparks.
- Many players can add to the same wall or tower.
- Big towers need lots of players working together.
- Builders build fastest. But anyone can help.

### Things dwarves can build

- Stone walls.
- Wood walls (cheap, but they burn).
- Gates that open and close.
- Archer towers.
- Spike traps.
- Pit traps.
- Oil pots that pour on monsters.
- Fix-it stations.
- Healing stations.
- Forges for weapons and armor.
- Kitchens for food.
- Potion tables.
- Stairs and ladders.
- Bridges.
- Quick walls for fast blocking.
- Flags and banners (just for looks).

### The castle grows in stages

- Stage 1: The Camp. A shrine, a workshop, a few fences.
- Stage 2: The Town. Walls, a gate, a tower or two, some traps.
- Stage 3: The Castle. Stone walls, many towers, gates behind gates, a grand look.

### Building rules

- No one player can build the whole castle alone.
- Working together is always faster than working alone.
- Every piece can break. Nothing is unbreakable.
- Gates break. Wood burns. Towers crack.
- You have to choose. Spend on the outer walls or on the shrine? You cannot do both.
- The castle resets every match. Every match is a fresh build.
- Building must be easy on a phone. Big buttons. Clear ghost shapes. Snap to grid.
- Keep the piece count low so phones do not lag.

## 8. Dwarf Jobs

### Builder

- Builds walls and towers fast.
- Fixes broken things.
- Places traps.
- Weak in a fight. Needs friends to guard them.

### Miner

- Digs gold, stone, and wood fast.
- Finds rare gems.
- Can dig short tunnels.
- Keeps the team rich.

### Fighter

- Big hammer or axe. Big shield.
- Holds the gate.
- Pushes monsters back.
- Guards builders and archers.

### Archer

- Shoots from walls and towers.
- Picks off monsters before they reach the gate.
- Fast but easy to hurt.

### Healer

- Heals friends.
- Puts a shield on walls for a short time.
- Slows monsters in one spot.
- Can speed up building a little.

### Jobs we can add later

- Smith (makes better armor and weapons).
- Potion Maker (makes potions and bombs).
- Baker (makes food that heals and helps).
- Cloth Maker (makes capes and light armor).
- Scout (fast runner, spots monsters early).

### Dwarf job rules

- Every job must matter during the build AND during the fight.
- A Builder still matters when the fight starts. They fix walls while monsters attack.
- You can switch jobs at a job station before the plague.
- No job is the "best." Each one is good at one thing.
- Every job has 2 or 3 moves. Not 10. Keep it simple.

## 9. Monster Jobs

### Grunt

- Basic fighter.
- Easy to learn.
- Strong in a big group.
- Most monsters start here.

### Brute

- Huge and slow.
- Smashes gates and walls.
- Takes lots of hits.
- Scary if you ignore it.

### Spitter

- Spits goo from far away.
- Knocks archers off walls.
- Hurts builders from far away.

### Witch

- Heals monsters close by.
- Makes walls weaker for a short time.
- Calls little helpers.
- Needs friends to guard it.

### Breaker

- Carries a big log or a bomb.
- Breaks gates very fast.
- Slow and easy to beat up close.

### Monsters we can add later

- Sneak (creeps in, breaks traps).
- Jumper (jumps over low walls).
- Digger (digs under walls).
- Boss monsters for special waves.

### Monster job rules

- Each monster has its own shape. You know what it is before you read its name.
- Each monster has one clear job. Break walls. Hurt archers. Heal friends. Rush the shrine.
- No one monster can beat the whole dwarf team alone.
- Monsters get stronger the longer they fight.
- Monsters come back after they die. But not too fast. Dying should still hurt a little.
- Monsters level up during the match. A grunt can become something scarier.

## 10. Dying and Switching Sides

- When a dwarf dies, they turn into a monster.
- They do not sit out. Ever.
- They pick a monster job right away.
- They start at the monster spawn.
- They know the weak spots in the castle. That is the twist.
- The dwarf team gets smaller. The monster team gets bigger.
- This makes the end of the match wild and fun.

How we keep it fair:

- The plague does not turn too many dwarves at once.
- New monsters start strong enough to matter.
- Dwarves get comeback tools. Fast fixes. Quick walls.
- Shrines take time to drain. Dwarves can fight back.
- We test the numbers a lot.

## 11. Who Wins?

Dwarves "win" when:

- They hold the last shrine until the clock runs out.
- Or they hold on for a very long time and beat tons of monsters.
- Holding on a long time is the real dwarf win. We make it a big deal.

Monsters win when:

- They drain the last shrine.
- Or every dwarf is dead.

Fair play rules:

- Monsters are supposed to win most matches in the end. That is how the real DvZ works.
- But every minute the dwarves hold on should feel earned.
- Good dwarves should hold Shrine 1 for a long time.
- Monsters should have to work as a team.
- We track how long dwarves last on average.
- We track which jobs win and lose the most.
- We fix anything that feels unfair.

## 12. The First 5 Minutes (Super Important)

New players must know EXACTLY what to do. No waiting. No big walls of words.

**0:00 - You spawn**

- You land inside a castle that is being built.
- Dwarves are carrying rocks. Walls are going up.
- A big sign says: HELP BUILD THE CASTLE.
- You can move right away. No movie to sit through.

**0:10 - Your first job**

- A glowing rock pile is close by.
- A trail of sparkles leads you there.
- You tap it. You get stone. A number pops up.
- It feels good.

**0:30 - Your first win**

- You carry stone to a glowing build spot.
- A wall rises up. Dust. Sparks. Hammer sound.
- A message says: You helped build this!

**1:00 - Your first choice**

- Three big buttons pop up.
- Make the gate stronger? Build a tower? Make the wall thicker?
- Each button has one short line. Not a paragraph.

**2:00 - The first scare**

- The sky gets a little darker.
- A horn blows.
- You see monsters far away on a hill.
- Now you know why you are building.

**3:00 - Your first fight**

- One small monster scout attacks.
- You swing. It flinches. It falls.
- Fighting feels good and clear.

**4:00 to 5:00 - You get it**

- You see the whole castle your team built.
- You see the three shrines.
- You see the plague clock.
- You want to see what happens next.

Rules for the first 5 minutes:

- Something fun happens every 15 to 30 seconds.
- No long lessons. Learn by doing.
- No standing around waiting for the next match.
- Players who join during build time jump right in.
- Players who join during the fight get a small job right away.
- Late players never get an unfair edge.

What we count:

- How many players leave in the first minute.
- How many players grab their first rock.
- How many players place their first wall.
- How many players reach their first fight.
- How many players finish a whole match.
- How many players come back the next day.

## 13. Animations

Animations are not the last thing we add. They are a core part of the game.

Moving around:

- Standing still, walking, running, jumping, landing, turning.
- Carrying heavy stuff and looking heavy.
- Climbing ladders.
- Tired breathing when low on health.
- Big armor changes how you move a little.

Fighting:

- A wind-up before a big swing.
- A clear arc for every weapon.
- Monsters flinch when hit.
- Push back that feels real.
- Blocking. Dodging. Stumbling.
- Sound and screen shake on every hit.

Building:

- Rocks fly into place.
- Wood frames pop up.
- Walls rise from the ground.
- Gates swing open and slam shut.
- Walls crack and crumble when broken.
- Upgrades change the look right away.

Monsters:

- Each one moves in its own way.
- Brute stomps. Spitter skitters. Witch floats.
- Clear wind-ups so dwarves can react.
- Fun death and defeat animations.

Big moments:

- The plague: sky changes, horn blows, dwarves turn.
- A shrine falling: a flash, a shake, a sad sound.
- A gate breaking: splinters, dust, a roar.
- The last stand: music gets faster, lights get redder.
- Win and lose poses for both teams.

Animation rules:

- A few great animations beat hundreds of okay ones.
- Reuse animation sets across jobs where we can.
- Blend animations so nothing snaps.
- Let phones turn down effects. Dust, shake, and sparks must be optional.

## 14. Art and Look

- Bright. Full of color. Happy, even when monsters attack.
- Chunky, friendly dwarves with big beards and big hammers.
- Monsters that look cool, not gross.
- Big shapes you can read on a tiny phone screen.
- The castle should look GRAND when it is done. Towers. Flags. Bridges.
- Sunset and night light that changes as the match goes on.
- Not Minecraft blocks. Smooth, cartoon style 3D.

Phone rules:

- Simple shapes. Not too many tiny details.
- Reuse castle pieces. Walls are made from the same few parts.
- Far-away things use low detail models.
- Hide things the player cannot see.
- Few shadows. Few see-through parts. Few sparkles.
- Test on cheap phones early. Not just on a gaming PC.

## 15. Sound and Music

- Happy, bouncy music during build time.
- A loud horn when the plague comes.
- Dark, fast drums during the attack.
- Music speeds up at the last stand.
- Hammer clinks when building.
- Big thuds when hitting.
- Monster growls you can tell apart.
- A big "shrine lost" sound everyone hears.
- A voice or text call-out: "Shrine 1 is falling!"
- Short, clear sounds for phones. Not too many at once.

## 16. Controls (Phone First)

- Joystick on the left. Buttons on the right.
- One big button for your main move. Swing, shoot, or build.
- One button for your special move.
- One button to switch between build mode and fight mode.
- Tap a build spot to help build it.
- Tap a job station to change jobs.
- Big buttons. No tiny icons.
- Everything you can do on PC, you can do on a phone.
- Short hints on screen the first time you do something.

## 17. Progress Between Matches

Your progress:

- Levels for your account.
- Levels for each job.
- Coins from every match.
- Titles like "Wall Master" or "Gate Breaker."
- Badges for cool things you did.

Things you unlock:

- Dwarf armor looks.
- Monster skin looks.
- Weapon and hammer looks.
- Build effects (gold sparkles when you place a wall).
- Emotes and dances.
- Win poses.
- Flags for the castle.

Progress rules:

- Nothing you unlock makes you stronger in a fight.
- A brand new player can beat an old player. Skill wins.
- The castle resets every match. Nobody starts with a built castle.
- Old players get more cool looks. Not more power.

## 18. Making Money (The Fair Way)

Things we sell:

- Dwarf skins.
- Monster skins.
- Weapon and hammer skins.
- Build effects.
- Emotes.
- Win poses.
- Castle flags and flag colors.
- Job look packs.
- Season look packs.
- A season pass with only looks in it.

Things we NEVER sell:

- Stronger jobs.
- More health or damage.
- Faster building.
- Skipping the plague.
- Safety from dying.
- Anything that lets you beat other players.

Money rules:

- The game must be fun for free.
- Buying stuff makes YOU look cool. It does not hurt others.
- No pop-ups during a match.
- The shop is easy to find but never in your face.

## 19. Friends and Teams

- Join with friends in a group.
- Groups stay on the same team at the start.
- Easy "Join Friend" button.
- Team chat and quick pings ("Help here!" "Gate is down!").
- Point at a spot to mark it for your team.
- End of match screen shows who did the most.
- A hangout room where you can show off skins between matches.
- Public matches and private matches with friends.

## 20. Keeping Players Coming Back

- Daily login gifts (coins for looks).
- Daily tasks like "Build 10 walls" or "Beat 5 monsters."
- Weekly challenges.
- A new season every 2 or 3 months with new skins.
- New maps over time.
- New monster and dwarf jobs over time.
- Special event weeks (double coins, giant boss waves).
- Top player lists for longest time holding on.
- Clubs for friends later on.

## 21. Safety and Fair Play

- Follow all Roblox rules for kids.
- Use Roblox chat filters.
- Easy report button.
- The game server decides every fight. Not the player's phone. This stops cheaters.
- Cheat checks on speed and damage.
- Kick players who quit matches early too often.
- No player can wreck their own team's castle on purpose.

## 22. Tech Plan

- Built in Roblox Studio.
- The server decides all fights and all building. Phones and PCs just show it.
- The castle is made from a small set of reused pieces.
- Low detail models for far-away stuff.
- Smart loading so phones do not choke.
- Save player progress with Roblox's save system.
- Keep the piece count under a set limit per match.
- Test with a full server of 24 players before launch.
- Test on cheap phones every week.

## 23. The Team and Tools

- Roblox Studio for the game.
- AI coding tools to write scripts faster.
- Blender or Roblox tools for 3D models.
- An animator or AI animation help for movement.
- A sound library and a few custom sounds.
- A simple task board to track work.
- A test group of 20 to 30 real players.

## 24. Build Order

**Phase 1: Lock the Design** — Finish this plan. Draw the first map on paper. Write down every job move. Write
down every rule for shrines and the plague. Pick the final name.

**Phase 2: Gray Test Version** — One ugly map with plain gray shapes. Players join and get put on a team. A build
clock. The plague turns some players. Simple fighting. Three shrines that can be taken. Dead dwarves become
monsters. Goal: Is the core loop fun? Yes or no.

**Phase 3: Building** — Mining for gold, stone, and wood. Snap-to-grid building. Walls, gates, towers, and traps.
Breaking and fixing. Shared build spots.

**Phase 4: Jobs** — 5 dwarf jobs. 5 monster jobs. Each with 2 or 3 moves. Job stations. Monsters level up during
the match.

**Phase 5: Make It Pretty** — Real art. Bright and full of color. Great animations. Sound and music. Phone
controls. The first 5 minutes. Big moment effects (plague, shrine falls, last stand).

**Phase 6: Test and Launch** — Small test with real players. Fix anything confusing. Count the first 5 minutes
numbers. Fix the fair play numbers. Make the game picture and icon. Launch to everyone. Watch the numbers every day.

**Phase 7: Grow** — New maps. New jobs. Seasons and skins. Events. Clubs.

## 25. The Game Picture and Icon

The game picture shows three things:

- A big, bright, amazing castle.
- A monster army charging at it.
- A few dwarves at the gate in one big action moment.

Game picture rules:

- No small words.
- No tiny people. Everything must show up on a phone.
- No mess.
- Bright colors that pop next to other games.

Test it:

- Make 3 versions. One about building. One about the attack. One about the last stand.
- Roblox lets us show more than one at a time.
- Keep the one that gets the most clicks AND keeps players.

## 27. Biggest Risks

- Fair play. If dwarves feel doomed, they quit. If monsters feel weak, they quit.
- Boring build time. If building feels like waiting, players leave.
- Phone lag. A big castle can crash a cheap phone.
- Confusing first minutes. New players must get it fast.
- Team wreckers. Players breaking their own team's stuff.
- Too much too soon. We must ship a small fun game first. Not a huge one.

How we fight each risk:

- Test fair play every week with real players.
- Make building feel like a game with fast rewards.
- Set a piece limit and test on cheap phones.
- Build the first 5 minutes with care and count the numbers.
- Block team damage and add a report button.
- Follow the build order. Prove the loop is fun before adding more.

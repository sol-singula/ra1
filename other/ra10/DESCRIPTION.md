# Farm to Fleet: the store description

Roblox shows only the first line or two in search and on the game tile, so the hook goes first. Every word is
plain enough for a third grader.

## Paste this

```
Plant one strawberry. Build a shipping empire! 🍓🚚

🌱 START SMALL
A shack, three patches of dirt and a farm stand. Plant, harvest, sell, and buy more land.

🐔 GROW A REAL FARM
Big fields, a barn, a tractor that harvests for you, and chickens, pigs, sheep and cows that make everything better.

📦 SHIP IT
Build a packing shed and fill orders. Then build a giant warehouse with racks, forklifts and trucks. Find the
slowest station (the red one!) and fix it to grow faster.

🚛 GO BIG
Dock doors, semis backing up to your warehouse, a control tower, a market hall. Your farm takes over the whole plot!

⭐ SPECIAL ORDERS
A gold truck pulls up with a rush order. Pack it fast for a huge payout and Gems!

🏁 WATCH YOUR STREET
Six farms side by side. The big board over the road shows who ships the most. Visit, like, and beat your neighbours!

🌴 FRANCHISE
Sell your company for Stars and start again in a richer place: Tropical Coast, Northern Dairy, Mega Port!

📅 Come back every day for rewards. Your farm keeps working while you're away!
```
